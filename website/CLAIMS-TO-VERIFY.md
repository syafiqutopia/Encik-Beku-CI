- **Our Story (brief §6.3)** — omitted. No confirmed founding history, milestones
  or expansion narrative was available, and §6.3 warns against inventing them.
  Supply the history and the section can be written.
- **Separate Price List (brief §17)** — not yet produced. Pricing currently lives
  in the combined Service Catalogue and on the What we do page, effective-dated.
- **Hero photograph** — currently a stock image. Brief §4 and §24 ask for real
  Encik Beku technicians in official uniform. Replace before launch, and confirm
  the licence on the present image if it stays.
- **State flags on the locations list** — hand-drawn as small SVG swatches
  (`#flag-*` in `about.html`), simplified to quarters, cantons, crescents and
  stars for a 22×15 badge. Recognisable at that size, but not official artwork.
  Check them against each state's own reference before launch.
